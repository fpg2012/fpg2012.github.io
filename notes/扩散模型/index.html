前向过程

原始数据记为x0x_0x0​，前向过程qqq就是逐步加上不同强度的高斯噪声

q(xt∣xt−1)=N(xt;1−βtxt−1,βtI) q(x_t|x_{t-1}) = \mathcal N (x_t; \sqrt{1-\beta_t}x_{t-1}, \beta_t \bold I) q(xt​∣xt−1​)=N(xt​;1−βt​​xt−1​,βt​I)

也就是说，xt=1−βtxt−1+βtϵtx_t = \sqrt{1-\beta_t}x_{t-1} + \sqrt{\beta_t}\epsilon_txt​=1−βt​​xt−1​+βt​​ϵt​，ϵt∼N(0,I)\epsilon_t \sim \mathcal N (0, \bold I)ϵt​∼N(0,I)

这一步就是某种扩散。βt\beta_tβt​反映了噪声的强度，至于xtx_txt​前面要乘上1−βt\sqrt{1-\beta_t}1−βt​​，应该是为了保持图像的“能量”总体上不变。可以给定x0x_0x0​，xtx_txt​

xt=(1−βt)(1−βt−1)…(1−β1))x0+βtϵt+βt−1ϵt−1+⋯+β1ϵ1 x_t = \sqrt{(1-\beta_t)(1-\beta_{t-1})\dots(1-\beta_1))}x_0 + \sqrt{\beta_t}\epsilon_t + \sqrt{\beta_{t-1}}\epsilon_{t-1} + \dots + \sqrt{\beta_1}\epsilon_1 xt​=(1−βt​)(1−βt−1​)…(1−β1​))​x0​+βt​​ϵt​+βt−1​​ϵt−1​+⋯+β1​​ϵ1​

每一步的噪声都是独立的。两个独立的高斯分布的线性组合还是高斯分布。

xt=∏(1−βt)x0+∏βtϵˉt x_t = \sqrt{\prod (1-\beta_t)} x_0 + \sqrt{\prod \beta_t} \bar\epsilon_t xt​=∏(1−βt​)​x0​+∏βt​​ϵˉt​

假设每一步增加的噪声都很小，方差的乘积可以忽略不计，可以近似写成。

q(xt∣x0)=N(xt;αˉtx0,(1−αˉt)I) q(x_t|x_0) = \mathcal N(x_t; \sqrt{\bar \alpha_t}x_0, (1-\bar \alpha_t)\bold I) q(xt​∣x0​)=N(xt​;αˉt​​x0​,(1−αˉt​)I)

其中αˉt=∏s=1t(1−βs)\bar \alpha_t = \prod^t_{s=1} (1-\beta_s)αˉt​=∏s=1t​(1−βs​)，这种写法可以简化后面的公式。

上面的公式表明，前向过程其实是个Markov过程，下个状态xtx_txt​只依赖上一个状态xt−1x_{t-1}xt−1​。联合分布可以直接写出来

q(x0:T)=q(x0)∏t=1Tq(xt∣xt−1) q(x_{0:T}) = q(x_0)\prod_{t=1}^{T} q(x_t|x_{t-1}) q(x0:T​)=q(x0​)t=1∏T​q(xt​∣xt−1​)

反向过程

直接假定反向过程的状态转移概率也是某种高斯分布，用参数为θ\thetaθ的神经网络拟合这个分布的均值和协方差。给定xtx_txt​，预测xt−1x_{t-1}xt−1​，用高斯分布去拟合xt−1x_{t-1}xt−1​在给定xtx_txt​之后的后验概率分布。

p(xt−1∣xt)=N(xt−1;μθ(xt,t),Σθ(xt,t)) p(x_{t-1}|x_t) = \mathcal N(x_{t-1}; \mu_\theta(x_t, t), \Sigma_\theta(x_t, t)) p(xt−1​∣xt​)=N(xt−1​;μθ​(xt​,t),Σθ​(xt​,t))

反向过程从xTx_TxT​出发，反向预测x0x_0x0​。因为加了很多噪声，可以认为p(xT)∼N(0,I)p(x_T) \sim \mathcal N(0, \bold I)p(xT​)∼N(0,I)

可见，也是Markov过程，联合分布同样可以直接写出来。

p(x0:T)=p(xT)∏t=1Tp(xt−1∣xt) p(x_{0:T}) = p(x_T)\prod_{t=1}^{T}p(x_{t-1}|x_t) p(x0:T​)=p(xT​)t=1∏T​p(xt−1​∣xt​)

损失函数

有个前向过程和反向过程，我们直接把x1:Tx_{1:T}x1:T​当作是隐变量，x0x_0x0​是显变量，直接套用ELBO作为优化目标，就能同时最大化似然函数，最小化KL散度。

Ex1:T∼q(x1:T∣x0)[log⁡p(x0∣x1:T)p(x1:T)q(x1:T∣x0)] \mathbb E_{\bold x_{1:T} \sim q(\bold x_{1:T}|\bold x_0)}\left[ \log \frac{p(\bold x_0 | \bold x_{1:T})p(\bold x_{1:T})}{q(\bold x_{1:T}|\bold x_0)}\right] Ex1:T​∼q(x1:T​∣x0​)​[logq(x1:T​∣x0​)p(x0​∣x1:T​)p(x1:T​)​]

把上面的联合分布公式代进去

Ex1:T∼q(x1:T∣x0)[log⁡p(xT)∏t=1Tp(xt−1∣xt)∏t=1Tq(xt∣xt−1)] \mathbb E_{\bold x_{1:T} \sim q(\bold x_{1:T}|\bold x_0)}\left[ \log \frac{p(x_T)\prod_{t=1}^{T}p(x_{t-1}|x_t)}{\prod_{t=1}^{T} q(x_t|x_{t-1})}\right] Ex1:T​∼q(x1:T​∣x0​)​[log∏t=1T​q(xt​∣xt−1​)p(xT​)∏t=1T​p(xt−1​∣xt​)​]

展开成积分

I1=∫q(x1:T∣x0)log⁡p(xT)∏t=1Tp(xt−1∣xt)∏t=1Tq(xt∣xt−1)dx1:T I_1 = \int q(x_{1:T}|x_0) \log \frac{p(x_T)\prod_{t=1}^{T}p(x_{t-1}|x_t)}{\prod_{t=1}^{T} q(x_t|x_{t-1})} \mathrm{d}x_{1:T} I1​=∫q(x1:T​∣x0​)log∏t=1T​q(xt​∣xt−1​)p(xT​)∏t=1T​p(xt−1​∣xt​)​dx1:T​

应用一下贝叶斯公式

q(xt∣xt−1)=q(xt−1∣xt)q(xt)q(xt−1) q(x_t|x_{t-1}) = \frac{q(x_{t-1}|x_t)q(x_t)}{q(x_{t-1})} q(xt​∣xt−1​)=q(xt−1​)q(xt−1​∣xt​)q(xt​)​

但是q(xt)q(x_t)q(xt​)、q(xt−1)q(x_{t-1})q(xt−1​)是没意义的，此时应该意识到x0x_0x0​要作为条件才对。

刚好根据Markov性，q(xt∣xt−1)=q(xt∣xt−1,x0)q(x_t|x_{t-1}) = q(x_t|x_{t-1}, x_0)q(xt​∣xt−1​)=q(xt​∣xt−1​,x0​)。所以

q(xt∣xt−1)=q(xt∣xt−1,x0)=q(xt−1∣xt,x0)q(xt,x0)q(xt−1,x0) q(x_t|x_{t-1}) = q(x_t|x_{t-1}, x_0) = \frac{q(x_{t-1}|x_t, x_0)q(x_{t}, x_0)}{q(x_{t-1}, x_0)} q(xt​∣xt−1​)=q(xt​∣xt−1​,x0​)=q(xt−1​,x0​)q(xt−1​∣xt​,x0​)q(xt​,x0​)​

代入上面的方程，神奇的事情就发生了

∫q(x1:T∣x0)log⁡p(xT)∏t=1Tp(xt−1∣xt)q(x1∣x0)∏t=2Tq(xt−1∣xt,x0)q(xt,x0)q(xt−1,x0)dx1:T \int q(x_{1:T}|x_0) \log \frac{p(x_T)\prod_{t=1}^{T}p(x_{t-1}|x_t)}{q(x_1|x_0)\prod_{t=2}^{T} \frac{q(x_{t-1}|x_t, x_0)q(x_{t}, x_0)}{q(x_{t-1}, x_0)}} \mathrm{d}x_{1:T} ∫q(x1:T​∣x0​)logq(x1​∣x0​)∏t=2T​q(xt−1​,x0​)q(xt−1​∣xt​,x0​)q(xt​,x0​)​p(xT​)∏t=1T​p(xt−1​∣xt​)​dx1:T​

有点乱，整理一下

∫q(x1:T∣x0)log⁡p(xT)∏t=1Tp(xt−1∣xt)∏t=2Tq(xt−1∣x0)q(x1∣x0)∏t=2Tq(xt−1∣xt,x0)∏t=2Tq(xt∣x0)dx1:T \int q(x_{1:T}|x_0) \log \frac{p(x_T)\prod_{t=1}^{T}p(x_{t-1}|x_t)\prod_{t=2}^{T}q(x_{t-1}| x_0)}{q(x_1|x_0)\prod_{t=2}^{T}q(x_{t-1}|x_t, x_0)\prod_{t=2}^{T}q(x_{t}|x_0)} \mathrm{d}x_{1:T} ∫q(x1:T​∣x0​)logq(x1​∣x0​)∏t=2T​q(xt−1​∣xt​,x0​)∏t=2T​q(xt​∣x0​)p(xT​)∏t=1T​p(xt−1​∣xt​)∏t=2T​q(xt−1​∣x0​)​dx1:T​

注意到，∏t=2Tq(xt−1∣x0)\prod_{t=2}^{T}q(x_{t-1}|x_0)∏t=2T​q(xt−1​∣x0​)就是q(x1∣x0)∏t=2T−1q(xt∣x0)q(x_1|x_0)\prod_{t=2}^{T-1}q(x_{t}| x_0)q(x1​∣x0​)∏t=2T−1​q(xt​∣x0​)，可以一下子消掉很多因子。

∫q(x1:T∣x0)log⁡p(xT)∏t=1Tp(xt−1∣xt)q(xT∣x0)∏t=2Tq(xt−1∣xt,x0)dx1:T \int q(x_{1:T}|x_0) \log \frac{p(x_T)\prod_{t=1}^{T}p(x_{t-1}|x_t)}{q(x_T|x_0)\prod_{t=2}^{T}q(x_{t-1}|x_t, x_0)} \mathrm{d}x_{1:T} ∫q(x1:T​∣x0​)logq(xT​∣x0​)∏t=2T​q(xt−1​∣xt​,x0​)p(xT​)∏t=1T​p(xt−1​∣xt​)​dx1:T​

拆成三项之和。

第一项，利用q(x1:T∣x0)=q(x1:T−1∣xT,x0)q(xT∣x0)q(x_{1:T}|x_0) = q(x_{1:T-1}|x_T,x_0)q(x_T|x_0)q(x1:T​∣x0​)=q(x1:T−1​∣xT​,x0​)q(xT​∣x0​)，改变积分顺序，再根据概率密度归一化的性质：

∫q(x1:T∣x0)log⁡p(xT)q(xT∣x0)dx1:T=∫q(xT∣x0)log⁡p(xT)q(xT∣x0)dxT=−KL(q(xT∣x0)∥p(xT))≜−LT \begin{aligned} & \int q(x_{1:T}|x_0)\log \frac{p(x_T)}{q(x_T|x_0)}dx_{1:T} \\ =& \int q(x_T|x_0)\log\frac{p(x_T)}{q(x_T|x_0)}dx_T \\ =& -\mathrm{KL}(q(x_T|x_0) \| p(x_T)) \triangleq -\mathcal L_T \end{aligned} ==​∫q(x1:T​∣x0​)logq(xT​∣x0​)p(xT​)​dx1:T​∫q(xT​∣x0​)logq(xT​∣x0​)p(xT​)​dxT​−KL(q(xT​∣x0​)∥p(xT​))≜−LT​​

第二项，地位相当于VAE当中的重构项。

∫q(x1:T∣x0)log⁡p(x0∣x1)dx1:T=∫q(x1∣x0)log⁡p(x0∣x1)dx1=Ex1∼q(x1∣x0)[log⁡p(x0∣x1)]≜−L0 \begin{aligned} &\int q(x_{1:T}|x_0)\log p(x_0|x_1)dx_{1:T} \\ =& \int q(x_1|x_0)\log p(x_0|x_1) dx_1 \\ =& \mathbb E_{x_1\sim q(x_1|x_0)}[\log p(x_0|x_1)] \triangleq -\mathcal L_0 \end{aligned} ==​∫q(x1:T​∣x0​)logp(x0​∣x1​)dx1:T​∫q(x1​∣x0​)logp(x0​∣x1​)dx1​Ex1​∼q(x1​∣x0​)​[logp(x0​∣x1​)]≜−L0​​

第三项

∫q(x1:T∣x0)log⁡∏t=2Tp(xt−1∣xt)q(xt−1∣xt,x0)dx1:T=∑t=2T∫q(x1:T∣x0)log⁡p(xt−1∣xt)q(xt−1∣xt,x0)dx1:T=∑t=2T∫q(xt−1∣xt,x0)log⁡p(xt−1∣xt)q(xt−1∣xt,x0)dxt−1=−∑t=2TKL(q(xt−1∣xt,x0)∥p(xt−1∣xt))≜−∑t=2TLt−1 \begin{aligned} & \int q(x_{1:T}|x_0)\log \prod_{t=2}^{T} \frac{p(x_{t-1}|x_t)}{q(x_{t-1}|x_t,x_0)}\mathrm{d}x_{1:T}\\ =&\sum_{t=2}^T\int q(x_{1:T}|x_0)\log\frac{p(x_{t-1}|x_t)}{q(x_{t-1}|x_t,x_0)}\mathrm{d}x_{1:T} \\ =& \sum_{t=2}^T\int q(x_{t-1}|x_t, x_0)\log\frac{p(x_{t-1}|x_t)}{q(x_{t-1}|x_t,x_0)}\mathrm{d}x_{t-1} \\ =& -\sum_{t=2}^T \mathrm{KL}(q(x_{t-1}|x_t, x_0) \| p(x_{t-1}|x_t)) \triangleq -\sum_{t=2}^T\mathcal L_{t-1} \end{aligned} ===​∫q(x1:T​∣x0​)logt=2∏T​q(xt−1​∣xt​,x0​)p(xt−1​∣xt​)​dx1:T​t=2∑T​∫q(x1:T​∣x0​)logq(xt−1​∣xt​,x0​)p(xt−1​∣xt​)​dx1:T​t=2∑T​∫q(xt−1​∣xt​,x0​)logq(xt−1​∣xt​,x0​)p(xt−1​∣xt​)​dxt−1​−t=2∑T​KL(q(xt−1​∣xt​,x0​)∥p(xt−1​∣xt​))≜−t=2∑T​Lt−1​​

总的loss如下，最小化这个loss就是最大化ELBO。

L=∑t=0TLt \mathcal L = \sum_{t=0}^T \mathcal L_t L=t=0∑T​Lt​

至此loss当中的各项都有明确的意义。Lt\mathcal L_tLt​就代表ttt步对应的损失。

这个损失函数是可以计算的。唯一一个看起来没法直接算出来的是q(xt−1∣xt,x0)q(x_{t-1}|x_t, x_0)q(xt−1​∣xt​,x0​)，但是我们知道q(xt−1∣x0)q(x_{t-1}|x_0)q(xt−1​∣x0​)和q(xt∣x0)q(x_{t}|x_0)q(xt​∣x0​)，用一下条件概率公式就能算出q(xt−1∣xt,x0)q(x_{t-1}|x_t, x_0)q(xt−1​∣xt​,x0​)。另外，这两个都是参数已知的高斯分布，所以其实条件分布的参数可以直接套公式算出来。

从概念上看，前向是x0→x1→⋯→xTx_0 \rightarrow x_1 \rightarrow \dots \rightarrow x_Tx0​→x1​→⋯→xT​，反向则是反过来。如果不看第一次扩散，变成从x1x_1x1​出发，前向是x1→x2→⋯→xTx_1 \rightarrow x_2 \rightarrow \dots \rightarrow x_Tx1​→x2​→⋯→xT​，这是可看作另一个扩散过程，起点是x1x_1x1​。如果我们递归地进行思考，照理说上面的ELBO应该可以继续拆分。并且，参考VAE的ELBO，也应该有重构项Eq[p(x0∣x1)]\mathbb E_q[p(x_0|x_1)]Eq​[p(x0​∣x1​)]。

我们应该可以任意地把整个过程的链条从中间某个位置拆开，切开的位置是显变量，右边是隐变量，左边是条件。但是推了半天，还是得从答案出发硬凑出一个结果，推导好像不是特别直观。
